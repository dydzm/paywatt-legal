Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Data We Collect

PayWatt is a local calculator. Your electricity usage inputs, saved calculations, contract details (utility, tier, wattage), and language preferences are stored on your device and are not transmitted to Wovex servers.

The following categories of data are collected automatically by third-party SDKs bundled with the app:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) — ad identifiers, approximate location (coarse, based on IP), device type, and ad-click interactions. Used to deliver and measure advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's partner data practices.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymous usage events (feature taps, session duration, app version, device model, language, country). Event parameters are bucketed before transmission (e.g. monthly usage is recorded in ranges like "300-500 kWh", never the exact reading). See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) / App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — your consent choice for personalized ads is stored locally and passed to Google Consent Mode v2. If you decline, non-personalized ads are served and Firebase Analytics uses modeled data only.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other direct identifier through the Application itself.
  • We do not read your exact electricity-bill amounts on our servers. Dollar/won/yen totals you compute stay on the device.
  • We do not sell Personal Data to third parties.

Use of Your Personal Data

The limited data collected by the bundled SDKs is used for:

  • Providing and maintaining the Service.

  • Delivering in-app advertising (banner, interstitial, rewarded) and measuring ad effectiveness.
  • Detecting crashes and performance issues.
